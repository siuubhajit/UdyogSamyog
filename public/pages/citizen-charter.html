⚖️ Statutory Citizen Rights & Deemed Clearance Provisions

1. Simultaneous Scrutiny Mandate

Under the Maharashtra Single Window Clearance Act, Phase 2 approvals (MIDC Land Planning, DISH Factory Safety, and Fire Services) must be evaluated concurrently within a parallel window of 21 days rather than sequentially.

2. Statutory Deemed Approval

If any regulatory department fails to communicate a decision or statutory deficiency notice within 15 working days, the Lead Approving Officer (Maharashtra State Innovation Society / Industries Apex Authority) is empowered to issue a deemed sanction.

3. One-Time Deficiency Protocol

Statutory scrutiny bodies are legally barred from raising piecemeal queries. All observations must be consolidated into a single formal deficiency notice providing the applicant a 7-day cure window.
