🏛️ How Single-Window Approvals Work

Three statutory phases: Phase 1 Environmental → Phase 2 Civil, Safety & Fire → Phase 3 Apex Final Sanction.

🌿 Phase 1: Environmental Review (MPCB)

Pollution Control Board reviews effluent sizing, hazardous waste management, and chimney emissions to issue Consent to Establish.

🏗️ Phase 2: Civil & Infrastructure (MIDC)

Industrial Development Corporation examines site layout drawings, Floor Space Index (FSI ≤ 1.0), and municipal road access.

⚙️ Phase 2: Factory Safety (DISH)

Directorate of Industrial Safety & Health verifies machine guarding, hazardous containment, and minimum 14.2 m³ worker air space.

🚒 Phase 2: Fire Prevention (Fire Services)

Maharashtra Fire Services certifies underground water storage reservoirs, ring-main hydrants, and 6m perimeter tender access.

🏛️ Phase 3: Consolidated Final Sanction (MSINS Apex Lead Authority)

Directorate of Industries & State Innovation Society synthesizes confirming clearances from MPCB, MIDC, DISH, and Fire Services to grant the final consolidated industrial license certificate.
